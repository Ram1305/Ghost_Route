NOTES FOR THE REVIEWER (paste into "Notes" field in ASC)
---------------------------------------------------------
Thank you for reviewing Ghost Route. Here is a complete guide to all
key flows:

── FIRST LAUNCH ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The app opens directly to the Home screen. No account, no subscription,
and no registration is required to enter the app. Servers can be viewed
from Home → bottom nav "Servers" or the "View All →" link on the home
screen.

── VPN CONNECTION (requires subscription) ────────────────────────────
1. Home screen → tap the power orb (shield icon)
2. If no active subscription → Premium screen opens automatically
3. On the Premium screen choose any of the three paths below

── PURCHASE WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT (Guideline 5.1.1(v)) ─────────────────
1. Home → tap power orb → Premium screen
2. Tap "Choose plan" → plan picker sheet
3. Select any plan → tap "Continue"
4. Dialog appears with three options:
   a. "Purchase now"  ← opens Apple payment sheet immediately,
                          NO account or registration required
   b. "Sign in"       ← for existing account holders
   c. "Create account"← optional account creation
5. Tap "Purchase now" → Apple payment sheet → complete purchase
6. "Payment successful" screen → tap "Go to Home"
7. Home → tap power orb → VPN connects

── CREATE ACCOUNT (Guideline 2.1(a)) ────────────────────────────────
Three separate entry points — no OTP, no email verification:

  A. Bottom nav "Account" → "Don't have an account? Create account"
  B. Bottom nav "Premium" → "New here? Create account"
  C. Purchase dialog → "Create account"

All lead to a simple form: username, email, password.
Account is created immediately upon tapping "Sign up".

── SIGN IN ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bottom nav "Account" → "Sign in"
Also accessible from: Premium screen → "Already have an account? Sign in"
Also accessible from: purchase dialog → "Sign in"

── RESTORE PURCHASES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bottom nav "Premium" → "Restore purchases"
(Requires sign in; after successful restore → navigates to Home)

── ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1) ───────────────────────────────
Sign in → bottom nav "Account" → scroll to bottom → "Delete account"
Enter password → confirm → account permanently deleted → Login screen

── SUBSCRIPTION & IAP (Guideline 3.1.1) ─────────────────────────────
All subscriptions go through Apple In-App Purchase only.
No external payment links or third-party payment processors.
Plans: Platinum and Platinum+ × Weekly / Monthly / Yearly (6 products)
Product IDs: see STORE_PRODUCTS.md

── PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS (Guideline 3.1.2) ─────────────────────────
Links appear at the bottom of every subscription/plan screen.
Privacy Policy: https://ghostroutes.netlify.app/
Terms of Use:   https://ghostroutetermsofuse.netlify.app/
Profile screen also has a dedicated "Privacy policy" button.

── VPN DATA COLLECTION (Guideline 5.1.2) ────────────────────────────
Q: What user information is collected via VPN?
A: The VPN tunnel collects NO user data. It does not log browsing
   activity, DNS queries, IP addresses of visited sites, or network
   traffic. The only personal data our backend collects is:
   · Email – account login and transactional emails only
   · Username – display name, used in-app only
   · Phone (optional) – not used for marketing
   · Subscription records – to verify active plan and restore purchases

Q: Why is this data collected?
A: Solely for account management, subscription verification, and
   customer support. No advertising or analytics use.

Q: Is this data shared with third parties?
A: No. Apple handles all payments via In-App Purchase. Our self-hosted
   backend has no advertising, analytics, or data-broker SDKs.
   We do not use ATT and do not track users across apps or websites.
