VPN DATA COLLECTION (Answers for Guideline 2.1 — paste in ASC reply)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What user information is the app collecting using VPN?

A: The VPN tunnel itself does not collect, monitor, or log any user
browsing activity, DNS queries, IP addresses of visited sites, or any
network traffic passing through the tunnel. The only personal
information we collect through our backend is:
  - Email address (required for account creation and login)
  - Username (display name chosen by the user)
  - Phone number (optional, collected at signup)
  - Subscription/transaction records (to verify and manage active plans)

Q: For what purposes is this information collected?

A: The data is collected solely for account management, subscription
verification, and customer support. Specifically:
  - Email: to identify the account, enable login, and send optional
    transactional emails (welcome, invoice, password reset).
  - Username: to personalise the in-app experience.
  - Phone: optional contact field; not used for marketing.
  - Subscription records: to verify active plan, enforce device limits,
    and handle restore-purchase requests.
  No VPN traffic or browsing data is ever collected or stored.

Q: Will the data be shared with any third parties?

A: No. We do not share, sell, or transfer user data to any third party.
  - Apple handles all payment processing through In-App Purchase; we
    only receive the App Store receipt for server-side verification.
  - Our backend is self-hosted (ghostroute.octosofttechnologies.in) and
    does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or data-broker SDKs.
  - We do not use any third-party analytics frameworks (e.g. Firebase
    Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude) in the app binary.

Thank you for your detailed feedback. We have addressed all four issues:

     tied to their Apple ID via StoreKit.

   • "Create account" — optional, allows users to sync the subscription
